FAQs

Questions, answered

How do I book a cleaner with NeatNest Cleaners?

You can book directly on our website or give us a call. Our team is always happy to assist.

Do I need to be home during the clean?

Not at all! Many of our clients provide secure access instructions and come back to a fresh, clean home.

What cleaning products do you use?

We bring our own eco-friendly and effective supplies unless you prefer we use your products.

Can I request the same cleaner each time?

Absolutely. We strive for consistency and are happy to assign your preferred cleaner whenever possible.

How much does cleaning cost?

Flexible packages start from as low as £15 per hour with no hidden costs. Need something custom? Ask for a free, no-obligation quote.
